# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Discuss behavior and code with concrete evidence;
do not attack people or disclose their private information. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and unsolicited contact are unacceptable.

AfterMe handles personal and emotionally sensitive writing. Do not demand
that users share letters, health details, bereavement information or identity
documents to obtain support. Use synthetic examples in discussions.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict access
when needed. Enforcement should be proportionate, explain the violated rule
and protect affected people's privacy. Maintainers handle moderation as their availability allows.

For a private concern, use the hosting platform's private reporting or
maintainer contact mechanism. If a safe private channel is unavailable, use
the platform's abuse-reporting process; do not post personal details publicly.
